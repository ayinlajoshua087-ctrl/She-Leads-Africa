SLA BoostHer Program

The SLA BoostHer Program, in collaboration with Jobberman is a transformative initiative designed to empower young Nigerian women entrepreneurs. This program addresses the unique challenges faced by women in business read the full article and job-seeking through a blended learning experience that includes advanced self-paced courses. Through a blended learning approach, participants will gain access to high-quality training, expert-led masterclasses, and hands-on guidance tailored to their needs, fostering sustainable business growth and economic independence.
